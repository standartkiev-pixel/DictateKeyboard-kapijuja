# Privacy Policy — Kapijuja Voice

Kapijuja Voice is an open-source keyboard. It does not operate a Kapijuja transcription proxy,
account system, advertising system, analytics service, or payment backend.

## Keyboard text

Ordinary typing is processed on the device. Kapijuja Voice does not send everything you type to a
Kapijuja server.

## Voice transcription and AI rewriting

When you choose an online AI provider, the recording or text required for the requested operation is
sent directly to the provider and endpoint configured on the device. The selected provider's own
privacy and retention rules apply.

If you select an on-device speech model, transcription stays on the device.

If you configure a custom or self-hosted endpoint, data is sent to the endpoint you entered.

## Stored data

Provider credentials, preferences, transcription history when enabled, dictionaries, and downloaded
models are stored using the application's Android storage. Android backup behavior follows the app's
backup configuration and the device's backup settings.

## Optional external services

GIF search uses KLIPY only when that feature is enabled and configured with a KLIPY API key. Search
requests are sent to KLIPY while the GIF panel is used.

Downloadable speech models and keyboard language assets may be fetched from their documented upstream
release hosting. Those downloads do not contain dictated text.

## No Dictate Cloud

The upstream Dictate Cloud credit service and its Google Play Billing purchase flow have been removed
from Kapijuja Voice. Kapijuja Voice does not route speech or payment information through
`api.dictatekeyboard.com`.
